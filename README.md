## Abstract

Customer support plays an important role in helping customers resolve issues related to products, orders, payments, refunds, and delivery services. Traditional customer support systems often require manual effort and may take more time to provide accurate responses. Generative AI can improve this process by automating customer queries and providing quick and intelligent assistance.

This project aims to compare three Generative AI approaches: Prompt Engineering, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and Fine-Tuning for customer support automation. The approaches will be evaluated based on response accuracy, response time, and cost to identify the most effective method. The proposed system aims to provide reliable customer support, reduce manual workload, and improve the overall customer experience.
